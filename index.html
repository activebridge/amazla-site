Amazla Key

Your watch is the key.

It pairs with your Tesla as a real Bluetooth phone key and signs every command with its own private key, which never leaves the watch.

Walk up, it opens

Keep the app open as you walk up. The status turns green “Authorized” and the handle just works.

Lock, frunk, trunk

One tap each, or map one to the watch button. Open lids show as open on screen.

Works with no signal

Underground garage, airplane mode, no data plan. Bluetooth is all it needs.

From the watch face

Shortcut card, key-card widget, and the free TeslaFace watch face with a one-tap key launcher.

Set it up once

Two presses to the trunk.

Pair at the car

A couple of minutes, once.

  1. Install from ZeppGet Amazla Key in the Zepp app and buy it on your phone. One payment covers up to 3 watches.
  2. Enter your carType the VIN in the app’s settings on your phone. Copy it from the Tesla app to be safe.
  3. Tap your key cardTap “Pair” on the watch, tap your key card on the console, then confirm the new key on the car’s screen.

Make it a fob

Open the trunk without touching the screen.

  1. Map a watch buttonOn the watch, open Settings → Preferences → Press Button and pick Amazla Key.
  2. Pick the actionIn the app’s settings, set Watch Button Action to Trunk, Frunk or Lock/Unlock.
  3. Press, pressThe first press opens the app, the second opens the trunk.

Faster still: turn on Remote Only (No Walk-Up). The watch skips the walk-up handshake, which takes 4 to 8 seconds off every connection. Door handles then no longer open the car by themselves.

FAQ

Good questions.

Which Teslas work?

Amazla Key works with any Tesla that supports a phone key over Bluetooth: Model 3, Model Y, Cybertruck, and Model S and Model X from 2021. Amazla works with any Tesla on your Tesla account.

Do I need my phone with me?

Not for Amazla Key. After pairing, the watch talks to the car on its own over Bluetooth. Amazla goes through the internet, so it uses your phone’s connection.

Does Amazla Key work in the background?

No. The app has to be open on the watch screen, because the watch does not allow Bluetooth from background apps. Map it to a watch button and it’s one press away.

Is it safe?

Amazla Key uses Tesla’s own phone-key protocol. Every command is signed on the watch with a key that never leaves it, and the car checks each one. Amazla signs in on Tesla’s own page and never sees your password.

Will Amazla wake my car and drain the battery?

Only when you tap. On Free and Plus the app never wakes the car on its own.

Is there a free trial of Amazla Key?

No. If it doesn’t work with your car, ask for a refund within 14 days and you get your money back.

Is it on Garmin?

Yes. Amazla Key is Tesla Key on the Garmin store, Amazla is Amazla(Tesla), and the watch face is TeslaKey-Link.

Something isn’t working. Who do I ask?

Open the app’s settings in the Zepp app and tap “Email us”, or write to contact@amazla.app.
